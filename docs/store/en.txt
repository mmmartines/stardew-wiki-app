TITLE: Valley Guide – Stardew Wiki
SHORT: Free, unofficial reader for the Stardew Valley Wiki, made by a fan.
FULL:
Look things up in the Stardew Valley Wiki on your phone, quickly and comfortably.

Valley Guide is an unofficial, free and non-profit app made by a fan to help the community. It shows the content of the Stardew Valley Wiki adapted for small screens: no more cut-off tables or pages that are hard to navigate.

WHAT YOU GET
• Sections menu: villagers, gifts, crops, fish, bundles, recipes, mines, weapons, festivals and much more
• Instant search by name and full-text search across every page
• Tables turned into cards, with a filter to find an item in long lists
• Collapsible sections, so you can jump straight to what you need
• Favorites and history, with offline reading of pages you already opened
• All 12 wiki languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Hungarian, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese
• Dark mode and adjustable text size
• Works from Android 7 to Android 16

ALWAYS FREE
No ads, no purchases and no donations. The app collects no data: favorites and history stay on your device.

READ-ONLY
The app only reads the wiki and never edits it. Every page links to the original article and to the history of who wrote it.
